University Capstone & Technical Documentation

Overview of CareLedger's B2B2C business model, 3-minute presentation script, and production engineering requirements for real healthcare deployment.

B2B2C Business Model

CareLedger is licensed as a subscription-based software platform sold directly to healthcare practices and hospital systems. Practices offer CareLedger to their patient panels to complement existing clinical workflows and nurse triage services.

  • Practice-Sponsored: Zero cost to patients; licensed per clinician seat.
  • Workflow Complement: Reduces unstructured patient portal message bloat by organizing symptoms into structured, pre-approved reports.
  • Clinical Safety: Human-in-the-loop triage ensures licensed nurses and physicians—never AI—evaluate medical urgency.

3-Minute Capstone Presentation Script

[0:00 - 0:45] The Problem & Patient Control: "Patients track daily health observations, but existing portals either lock data away or give providers intrusive full access. CareLedger introduces patient-controlled health diaries where raw tracking history remains strictly private."

[0:45 - 1:45] Live Demo - Diary & Report Builder: "Demonstrating our Tailwind frontend, patients log symptoms and lifestyle habits. In Tab 2, patients select specific date ranges and categories. Choosing between deterministic rule-based summaries and Gemini AI summarization, the system synthesizes trends without making diagnoses."

[1:45 - 2:30] Approval Gate & Provider Comm: "Crucially, export and provider communication remain locked until the patient clicks Approve Exact Report. Any edit instantly revokes approval. Once approved, patients can simulate a nurse triage review."

[2:30 - 3:00] Business Model & Production Gaps: "Licensed to clinics as a B2B SaaS tool, CareLedger bridges patients and care teams safely."

Production Engineering & Real Healthcare Requirements

1. Regulatory & Compliance

Requires HIPAA Business Associate Agreements (BAAs), SOC 2 Type II certification, and strict zero-knowledge end-to-end encryption for diary storage.

2. Secure AI & Backend

Moving beyond frontend session keys requires a secure serverless backend (Node.js/Python on AWS/Vercel) to protect API credentials and log audit trails.

3. EHR Interoperability

Integration with clinical electronic health record systems (Epic, Cerner) via HL7 FHIR APIs to securely ingest approved reports into provider charts.
