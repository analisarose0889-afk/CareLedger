CareLedger: Business Model, Demo Guide & Production Architecture

Documentation for evaluators and stakeholders detailing the B2B2C healthcare SaaS model, 3-minute live presentation walkthrough, and engineering requirements for real-world clinical deployment.

1 B2B2C Healthcare SaaS Business Model

Revenue Model (Practice-Sponsored Subscription): CareLedger is licensed via a recurring tiered SaaS subscription to outpatient medical practices, specialty clinics, and value-based care networks, who offer the platform free of charge to their enrolled patients.

Value to Clinics & Nurses
  • Structured, concise pre-visit summaries instead of scattered verbal recall
  • Reduces nurse triage intake call duration
  • Complements existing EHR and triage workflows without replacing clinicians
Value to Patients
  • 100% private personal diary—clinicians cannot snoop on raw entries
  • Granular category consent & explicit sign-off before sharing
  • Zero out-of-pocket cost to the patient

3 Required Services & Development for Real Healthcare Deployment

What separates this educational prototype from a production clinical product:

Technical Gap Analysis
1. HIPAA Compliance & Security Infrastructure

Requires signed Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) with cloud and AI vendors, end-to-end encryption (TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest), zero-data-retention AI inference endpoints, and immutable HIPAA audit logs.

2. Identity Verification & Zero-Knowledge Vault

Production deployment requires Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), OAuth2/OIDC patient identity proofing, and client-side cryptographic key management so even database administrators cannot read raw private diary notes.

3. EHR Interoperability & Clinical Safety

Requires HL7 FHIR R4 API integration (e.g., Epic, Cerner/Oracle Health) to route approved PDF/JSON reports into clinic triage queues, plus deterministic red-flag symptom detection that directs acute emergencies to 911.

How to Share & Obtain a Publicly Accessible Link

Because CareLedger is engineered as a self-contained single-file web application (index.html) with zero build steps, you can host it publicly in under 60 seconds using any free static host:

Option 1: GitHub Pages

Create a free GitHub repository, upload index.html, and enable Settings → Pages to receive an instant *.github.io public URL.

Option 2: Netlify Drop

Visit app.netlify.com/drop and drag-and-drop the folder containing index.html for an immediate shareable HTTPS link.

Option 3: Vercel / Canvas Share

Deploy via Vercel CLI/web upload or use your environment's built-in Share / Publish Preview link directly during your capstone pitch.
